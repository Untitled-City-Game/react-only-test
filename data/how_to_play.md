
Welcome to Playground City! Today you'll play a giant game of Connect Four where every square is an entire neighbourhood.

Make sure everyone reads these rules before you start.

## 🗺️ **The City is your game board**

Your city will be divided into a grid of **neighbourhoods**.

![alt text](/how_to_play/emptymap.png)

You'll be able to see both teams' locations on the map.

![alt text](/how_to_play/location_pins.png)

## ↔️↕️ **Be the first to make a line**

You win if you connect four neighbourhoods in a straight line, before the other team.

![alt text](/how_to_play/winner.png)

Winning lines are shown on the map when you select a neighbourhood.

![alt text](/how_to_play/line.png)

Some neighbourhoods overlap multiple lines, so be sure to check!

![alt text](/how_to_play/oddline.png)

## 🏆 **Challenges**

To claim a neighbourhood, go there and complete a challenge.

![alt text](/how_to_play/challenge.png)

When you complete a challenge, you'll need to send photos or videos proving you've done it.

![alt text](/how_to_play/evidence.png)

Your team will start with five challenges. When a challenge is completed, a new challenge is drawn.

You can also **discard** all your challenges to get five new ones. Be careful, if you run out, there's no more!

## 🔒 **Locking and stealing**
Usually, if a team claims a neighbourhood, the other team can't claim it back.

However, some challenges can **steal** a neighbourhood that is already claimed.

These challenges are a bit harder, and there's less of them.

![alt text](/how_to_play/hard.png)

If you complete one of these hard challenges, you **lock** that zone, and it can't be stolen, even by another hard challenge.

![alt text](/how_to_play/locked.png)

## **Ties**

If neither team completes a line within the time limit, the team with the most neighbourhoods wins.

If both teams have the same number of neighbourhoods, that's just a tie!

## **Tips and tricks**
Here's some general advice on how to play well.

### **You really have to be scrappy**

Blocking the other team's line and stealing their neighbourhoods are essential. If you don't do this, you'll probably lose!

So, don't be scared to throw some elbows. It's part of the fun!

### **Stay close to the other team**

All you need to win the game is four neighbourhoods. With the right combo of challenges and transit, this can happen really fast!

So, be cautious how far you get from the other team. If you're nearby, you can see them making a line, and maybe block them before they win.

### **Slow challenges are dangerous**

Some challenges might look really easy, but always consider the time commitment. While you're locked down for 10 or fifteen minutes, a lot can change!

## **Other important rules**

### **Stay together!**

Your team has to stay together. You can't split up and do different things, or to cover more ground!

### **Stay within neighbourhood!**

You have to stay inside the neighbourhood the whole time you're completing a challenge.


### **Find it during the game!**

If the challenge involves finding or making something, you can't use something you brought with you.

For example, if the challenge was "Find a sandwich" you couldn't use one from your bag.

### **Be respectful!**

None of the challenges require you to do anything disruptive, or make strangers uncomfortable! 

Don't be disruptive or annoying while playing, please.
